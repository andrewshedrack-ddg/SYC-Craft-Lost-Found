About this board

SYC Craft built this board because lost items on campus often stay lost for a simple reason: the person who found the item and the person who lost it never meet. This site puts both reports in one searchable place, with clear steps for claiming.

Who we are

We are SYC Craft, a two-person DBT 1305 team. Andrew Shedrack Anderson and Anita Mwaura are building this site from scratch as our Week 7 team project. Our Lab 2 sacco site stays separate and is not part of this project.

What the board does

  • Lists lost items with a description, place, and date.
  • Lists found items so owners can recognise them.
  • Explains claiming clearly, so no item is handed to the wrong person.
  • Works on a phone as well as on a computer, with no sideways scrolling.

What it does not do

This is a class project board, not a university lost property office. It does not hold items itself. Found items stay with the finder or at the desk named in the report until the owner claims them.

Need help fast?

Visit the student centre desk, Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm, with your student ID and a clear description of the item.

Board rule for this class project: bring proof of ownership when you claim an item.
