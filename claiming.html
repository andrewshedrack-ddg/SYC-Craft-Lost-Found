How claiming works

Claiming is kept simple and safe. The steps below are the board rules for this class project.

  1. Find a match. Check the browse page and compare the description, place, and date with your lost item.
  2. Contact through the board. Use the contact form and say which item you think is yours, without sharing private numbers in public.
  3. Prove it is yours. Bring your student ID and a detail only the owner would know, such as what was inside the wallet or a mark on the item.
  4. Meet in a public place. Collect the item at the student centre desk during opening hours, Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm.
  5. Confirm the handover. Both sides confirm the item has changed hands, so the report can be marked as claimed.

If no one claims an item

Found items listed here stay listed until claimed. If an item is still unclaimed after 30 days, the finder or desk should follow the usual campus guidance for unclaimed property, rather than keeping it as their own without asking.

Safety notes

  • Never share a bank card number, PIN, or password to prove ownership.
  • Do not meet a stranger alone to collect a valuable item.
  • If an item looks stolen or dangerous, report it to campus security instead of listing it here.

Need help fast?

Visit the student centre desk, Monday to Friday, 9am to 4pm, with your student ID and a clear description of the item.

Board rule for this class project: bring proof of ownership when you claim an item.
